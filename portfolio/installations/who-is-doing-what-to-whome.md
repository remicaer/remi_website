# Who is doing what to whom

2022 · Installation

"Who is doing what to whom" was an audio sculptural artwork, designed and fabricated for artist Thembi Soddell in 2022. The work was a 2m tall black cube, containing 16 speakers held in precise position to create an intangible spatial audio experience.

The installation was constructed out of steel tube and CNC cut aluminium composite and designed as a travelling work — one that could easily be packed down, transported and assembled quickly at any location.

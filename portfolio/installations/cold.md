# COLD

2019 · Installation

COLD was presented in September 2019 at the Yarra Sculpture Gallery in Melbourne, Australia. It is a collaborative artwork between sound artist Jordan Lacey and visual artist remi caer.

Together, they combine technology, phenomenology and ambiance theory to translate the sound and lightscape of the Victorian Alpine high country into a laneway adjacent to the gallery. The sounds of small eddies discovered along a creek tributary were recorded with a combination of ambisonic, hydrophone and contact microphones. These recordings are played back through four loudspeakers embedded inside bespoke air-conditioning shells, typical to the Melbourne laneway typology.

A 'new nature' is suggested by the metallic resonance of the water sounds, combined with flickering white LEDs that light up the shells' interiors.
